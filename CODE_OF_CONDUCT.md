# Code of conduct

Pedalator is for people who like riding and playing. Everyone is welcome here.

**We expect** everyone in the project's spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) to:

- be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to Bluetooth, Python or modding;
- assume good intent, and give constructive, specific feedback;
- accept that people use different hardware, languages and skill levels;
- keep discussion about the project.

**We do not accept** harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, publishing other people's private information, or spam.

**Enforcement.** Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, open an issue marked *conduct* without personal details, or contact the maintainer through the email on their GitHub profile.

This is a short version in the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
